Privacy policy

laptopguard is a personal tool, and only its owner uses it.

  • It uses the Google Drive drive.file scope, so it can reach only the files it creates in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • Backups are encrypted on the owner's computer before they are uploaded.
  • It collects no data from other people, and it shares no data with anyone.
  • It sends data only to the owner's Google Drive.
